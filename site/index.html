We reach and empower teenagers in Uganda through scholarships, school materials, fellowship and skills, and we partner with people and companies who want to invest in their future.

It began with what others left behind.

In 2022, our founder, Judith Shabamukama, noticed books, pens, soap and other useful school supplies being discarded each term. She began gathering them and sharing them with nearby communities, and that simple act grew into a larger mission of compassion, education and hope.

What we work on

Seven objectives guide everything we do.

Education

Supporting school attendance, learning, career growth, and access to practical opportunities.

Health awareness

Promoting healthy routines, practical care and informed choices for teenagers and families.

Skills and knowledge

Building practical understanding that helps young people become productive, resilient and confident.

Compassionate outreach

Meeting people where they are with dignity, empathy and consistent care.

Importance of community

Helping people see that they are not alone, and that support grows stronger when shared.

Local presence

Walking with communities through local engagement, practical help and long-term relationships.

Young people as future leaders

Investing today so the next generation can become thoughtful, capable contributors to society.

Projects & impact

Building continuity, not one-off aid.

Our mission is rooted in long-term community development: education, health awareness, food value addition, youth mentorship and practical support that meets real needs.

We take a steady, people-centered approach so that every contribution creates a lasting foundation for growth.
