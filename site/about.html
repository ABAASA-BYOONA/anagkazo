About us

How one person's concern grew into a registered organization.

It began with what others left behind.

In 2022, our founder, Judith Shabamukama, began noticing something at the end of every school term. In dormitories and classrooms, perfectly usable books, pens, scholastic materials, soap and other essentials were left behind or wasted. To someone else, they could be a much-needed resource.

She began collecting them and sharing them with nearby communities. Alongside distributing items, she reached out through evangelism, discovering that outreach is not only about giving people something they need, but reminding them that they are seen, valued and worthy of hope.

How the work has grown

  1. 2022Collecting and sharing books, pens, soap and other essentials.
  2. 2024First organized community outreach, reaching more than 100 people.
  3. 2025Bwaise outreach reached about 200 to 300 people with scholastic and essential materials.
  4. 2025 to 2031Our long-term community initiative focused on education, health and youth empowerment.

Registration was more than a legal milestone. It turned a small idea into an organization with a responsibility to serve, grow and reach more people.

Our vision

To minister mercy, compel souls, and raise a generation of winners through compassionate outreach.

Our mission

To reach and empower teenagers through scholarships, provision of scholastic materials, fellowships, spiritual and skills development, while partnering with individuals, organizations and companies to support their education and future.
